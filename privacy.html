Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Who we are

Casa Tinkuy is a Peruvian dining and cocktail project based in Las Vegas, Nevada. You can reach us at casatinkuylv@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you give us. If you join our list, we keep your email address. If you write to us, we keep your message and your email address so we can reply.

How we use it

To tell you about upcoming Casa Tinkuy events and to answer your messages. We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Who helps us

Our website is hosted by Netlify, which receives newsletter sign-ups on our behalf. When tickets are available, purchases are handled by our ticketing provider under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The site remembers your language choice, English or Spanish, in your browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads.

Your choices

To leave our list, or to ask what information we hold about you, email us and we'll take care of it.

Age

Our events are for guests 21 and over, and this site is not directed to anyone under 21.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
