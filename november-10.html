Encounter Nº 1 · Peru

This encounter has taken place. Thank you to every guest, partner and member of our team who made the first Casa Tinkuy evening possible. Photos and stories from the night are coming soon.

Las Vegas has some of the best restaurants and bars in the world. In most of them, Peru is missing: its ingredients, its spirits, its people. Casa Tinkuy sets a place for it at the table, with a kitchen and a bar that treat Peru as seriously as any cuisine in this city.

By reservation

The Dinner

Six courses by Chef Michael Palomino, built around ají amarillo, the yellow chili at the heart of Peruvian cooking, served in staggered seatings through the evening. Add a pairing of four drinks: Peruvian cocktails by Angelo Palomino with La Diablada Pisco, or a non-alcoholic pairing.

Dinner $128 per guest · Cocktail pairing $45 · Non-alcoholic pairing $30 · $50 deposit to reserve

Walk-ins welcome

The Bar

No reservation needed for cocktails. Pull up a seat at the bar for pisco cocktails made with Peruvian ingredients, and stay as the night turns into an after-hours lounge. Seven seats, first come, first served.

The six-course menu is served by reservation only. Food isn't available at the bar.

Good to know

Questions

Where is it?

Echo Taste & Sound, 1301 S Main St, Suite 160, Las Vegas, NV 89104, in the Arts District.

Google MapsApple Maps

Can I come just for the bar?

Yes. During dinner service, the bar is open to walk-ins for cocktails, no reservation needed. Space is limited: seven seats at the bar, first come, first served.

Can I order food at the bar?

The bar serves cocktails only. Chef Michael's six-course menu is served exclusively to dinner guests with a reservation.

Is it 21 and over?

Yes. Casa Tinkuy is a 21-and-over event, for both the dinner and the bar. Please bring a valid photo ID.

How do reservations work?

Reserve online with a $50 deposit per guest, fully refunded to your card after dinner. Dinner is $128 per guest, paid at the table. Pairings are optional: a cocktail pairing with La Diablada Pisco for $45, or a non-alcoholic pairing for $30, each with four drinks served at selected moments throughout the six courses. Tax and gratuity are not included. Cancel at least 72 hours ahead for a full refund. No-shows and late cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary needs?

Please tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you book, and we'll let you know what we can accommodate. The menu is built around ají amarillo, so mention it if you're sensitive to spice.

Any other questions?

Write to us at casatinkuylv@gmail.com.
