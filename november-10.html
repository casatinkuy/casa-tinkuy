Encounter Nº 1 · Peru

Las Vegas has some of the best restaurants and bars in the world. In most of them, Peru is missing: its ingredients, its spirits, its people. Casa Tinkuy sets a place for it at the table, with a kitchen and a bar that treat Peru as seriously as any cuisine in this city.

Ticketed

The Dinner

Six courses by Chef Michael Palomino, built around ají amarillo, the yellow chili at the heart of Peruvian cooking, with four Peruvian cocktails by Angelo Palomino, served in staggered seatings through the evening.

Walk-ins welcome

The Bar

No ticket needed. Come in for a Peruvian cocktail during dinner service, and stay for the after-hours lounge with live music. Six seats at the bar, plus a little standing room.

Questions

Where is it?

Echo Taste & Sound, 1301 S Main St, Suite 160, Las Vegas, NV 89104, in the Arts District.

Google MapsApple Maps

Can I come just for the bar?

Yes. During dinner service, the bar is open to walk-ins for cocktails, no ticket needed. Space is limited: six seats at the bar and a small standing area, first come, first served.

Is it 21 and over?

Yes. Casa Tinkuy is a 21-and-over event, for both the dinner and the bar. Please bring a valid photo ID.

When do tickets go on sale?

Soon. Follow @casatinkuylv on Instagram to hear first.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary needs?

Please tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you book, and we'll let you know what we can accommodate. The menu is built around ají amarillo, so mention it if you're sensitive to spice.
