The House

What Casa Tinkuy means

Spanish

casa

/ˈka·sa/ noun

A house. A home. The place you are welcomed into and fed, where the door stays open a little longer than it needs to.

Quechua

tinkuy

/ˈtiŋ·kuj/ noun

An encounter. The meeting point of people, rivers, traditions and opposing forces. In the Andes, a tinkuy is where two things come together and make something new.

The threshold

Every encounter begins at a door

Casa Tinkuy starts with a simple gesture: opening the door and inviting people in. What happens on the other side is the encounter.

Where the house travels

Pop-ups, residencies and collaborations, starting in Las Vegas

Casa Tinkuy starts in Las Vegas and is built to move, bringing Latin American chefs, mixologists and musicians together.
